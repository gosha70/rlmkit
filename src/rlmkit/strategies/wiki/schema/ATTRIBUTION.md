# Schema attribution

`page-types.md`, `ingest-rules.md`, `citation-rules.md`, and
`lint-rules.md` are copied verbatim from
`gosha70/code-copilot-team` (commit `f294bf6`,
`knowledge/wiki/schema/`). The schema is the validated taxonomy
for the Karpathy-pattern LLM Wiki and is reused unmodified so
that pages produced by `rlmkit wiki ingest` are interchangeable
with pages produced by code-copilot-team's `./scripts/wiki ingest`.

If you need to evolve the schema, propose the change in both
projects to keep them in sync.
